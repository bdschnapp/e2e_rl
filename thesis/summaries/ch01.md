# Ch1: Introduction — Summary

**Motivation**: Articulated tractor-trailer vehicles are complex control targets due to non-holonomic hitch constraints and reverse instability (jackknife). Classical methods (NMPC) are computationally demanding.

**Problem**: Two-part: (1) designing reward functions for simultaneous path tracking + hitch stability; (2) perception from high-dimensional BEV imagery rather than symbolic state.

**Contributions**:
1. Full kinematic + dynamic tractor-trailer model in Gymnasium/Pygame simulation
2. Vision-based observation space (84×84 BEV image + state vector) with CNN encoder
3. Reward function balancing CTE, heading error, articulation angle, collision avoidance
4. TD3 agents for lane-following and obstacle avoidance tasks
5. Comparison with PID, MPC baselines and ME780 DDPG results

**NOT claimed** (future work): Gazebo, ROS2, Agilex hardware, LIDAR, real-world deployment.

**Institutional context**: MASc, Dept. of Mechanical & Mechatronics Engineering, University of Waterloo. Relates to WATonoBus / MVS Lab.
