# Ch2: Literature Review — Summary

**Articulated vehicle control**:
- PID: simple, can't anticipate jackknife
- MPC (linear): hard constraints on γ, computationally feasible
- NMPC: full nonlinear kinematics, higher cost
- Active hitch stabilisation: extra DoF at hitch, not applicable to standard tractor-trailer

**RL in autonomous driving**:
- DDPG (ME780): off-policy actor-critic for continuous action; 0.505m CTE on single vehicle
- PPO: more stable than DDPG but lower sample efficiency
- Decision Transformer (ME780): offline RL via sequence modelling; 1.717m CTE, prone to oscillation with narrow training data

**Perception**:
- CNNs: standard for spatial feature extraction from BEV images in simulation
- Autoencoders: compress BEV images to latent vector z for RL efficiency; pre-trained on simulation rollouts

**Safety**:
- Jackknife is the primary safety concern; reward must penalise large |γ|
- CBFs: future work — formal hard safety overlay on learned policy

**Waterloo thesis context**: Recent MASc theses require strong modelling + comprehensive validation. "It works" is insufficient; need quantified stability limits and baseline comparisons.
