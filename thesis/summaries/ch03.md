# Ch3: Mathematical Modeling — Summary

**Tractor model**: Dynamic bicycle model with Pacejka Magic Tire approximation (linear for small slip angles). State: [ẋ, ẏ, ψ̇]. Inputs: [F_x, δ]. Same equations as ME780 and ECE682 prior work.

**Trailer model**: Kinematic model derived from hitch constraints. Hitch point at tractor rear axle (l_r behind CoM). Trailer axle length l_t. Articulation angle γ = ψ_tractor − ψ_trailer. Under no-lateral-slip assumption: γ̇ = (v_t / l_t) sin(γ).

**Reverse instability**: Sign reversal of forward velocity in kinematic equations turns stabilising effect into destabilising force. Episode terminates at |γ| > 90°.

**MDP formulation**:
- S: {vector observation, 84×84 BEV image}
- A: [δ̇, v] ∈ [-15°/s, 15°/s] × [0, 2 m/s]
- T: Pygame/Gymnasium 2D simulation (NOT Gazebo)
- R: see Ch5
- γ_d: discount factor

**Key equations** (in `equations/tractor_model.tex` and `equations/trailer_reverse_stability.tex`):
- Longitudinal/lateral/yaw dynamics (same as ME780 eqs 1-8)
- Zero-order-hold discretisation at dt=0.1s
- Trailer hitch kinematics
